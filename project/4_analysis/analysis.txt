CS 131 Phase 4 — Final Analysis

Research question:
How do trip duration, trip distance, pickup hour, and year relate to fare,
recorded tips, demand, and total trip cost in NYC Yellow Taxi trips from
January 2019 through May 2026?

Dataset:
89 monthly Parquet files
5,196,788,554 bytes
Approximately 5.20 GB
January 2019 through May 2026

Main findings:

1. Trip duration
Average recorded tips increased with trip duration.
Trips lasting 0-10 minutes averaged about $1.64 in recorded tips.
Trips lasting 31-45 minutes averaged about $5.96.
Trips lasting 61-180 minutes averaged about $8.16.

2. Trip distance
Average recorded tips increased with distance.
Trips under 1 mile averaged about $1.54.
Trips from 5-10 miles averaged about $4.32.
Trips over 20 miles averaged about $10.18.

3. Demand by hour
Taxi demand was lowest during the early morning.
Demand increased through the day and peaked near 6 PM.

4. Total trip cost
Longer and farther trips produced higher fares, tolls, and total amounts.
Trips over 20 miles averaged about $77.55 in fare and $97.73 in total cost.

Conclusion:
Distance and duration had strong positive relationships with recorded tip
amount and total trip cost. Pickup hour was most useful for identifying demand.
A self-driving taxi service could position more vehicles during afternoon and
evening demand peaks while using longer trips as higher-revenue opportunities.

Limitation:
The tip_amount field mainly reflects electronically recorded tips. Cash tips
may be missing, so near-zero cash tips do not mean cash passengers never tip.

Row count:
The Phase 4 analysis retained 320,753,342 valid trip records after filtering.
The filters removed trips with invalid or extreme values, including nonpositive
trip duration, trips longer than 180 minutes, distances above 100 miles, and
negative fare or total amounts.
